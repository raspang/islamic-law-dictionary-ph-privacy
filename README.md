 This privacy policy explains how the Android app Islamic Law Dictionary PH (package com.nzpenterprise.islamiclawdictionary), published by NZP Enterprise, handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.
Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
Internet access

The app works fully offline. It does not request internet permission, so it cannot send any data from your device to us or to anyone else.
Information stored on your device

To make the app useful, some information is saved only on your device:

    Bookmarks, favourite terms and chapters marked as read
    Recently viewed dictionary terms (search history)
    Study progress, quiz and mock exam results, and your exam date
    Reading settings such as text size and page colour

We cannot see or access this information. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's history in the app, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.
Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, your phone may include the app's saved settings and progress in your personal backup (for example, to your Google account). This backup is handled by Android and Google under your own account settings and Google's privacy policy. We do not receive or have access to it.
Sharing

The app has a Share button for dictionary terms. Text is shared only when you tap it, and only to the app you choose (for example, a messaging app). We do not receive anything you share.
Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.
Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
